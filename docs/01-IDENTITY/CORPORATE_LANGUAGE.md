# Corporate Language

> Defines the official institutional language framework of Breto's Holding Group.

---

# Purpose

Corporate Language establishes the terminology and communication principles used throughout the BHG ecosystem.

Its purpose is to preserve clarity, consistency and institutional identity across:

- companies;
- subsidiaries;
- products;
- repositories;
- documentation;
- human teams;
- artificial intelligence systems.

Language is considered an institutional asset.

---

# Language Principles

The BHG corporate language follows these principles:

## Precision

Communication shall prioritize clear and accurate concepts.

Ambiguous terminology shall be avoided.

---

## Consistency

Official terms shall maintain the same meaning across the ecosystem.

Different interpretations of institutional concepts are prohibited.

---

## Institutional Alignment

Communication shall reflect:

- BHG principles;
- governance structure;
- strategic vision;
- organizational identity.

---

## Long-Term Preservation

Language decisions shall consider future interpretation by:

- future employees;
- future leaders;
- external partners;
- artificial intelligence systems.

---

# Official Communication Style

BHG institutional communication shall be:

- professional;
- clear;
- structured;
- evidence-based;
- respectful;
- internationally understandable.

---

# Institutional Terminology

The following concepts have official meanings.

---

## Ecosystem

The complete network of organizations, technologies, assets, knowledge systems and governance structures belonging to BHG.

---

## Holding

The institutional structure that owns and coordinates multiple companies and strategic assets.

---

## Foundation

The conceptual and institutional layer preserving identity, principles and long-term continuity.

---

## Governance

The system through which authority, decisions and organizational evolution are controlled.

---

## Repository

A controlled digital environment containing institutional knowledge, software, documentation or operational assets.

---

## Document

A governed institutional artifact containing structured knowledge.

---

## Asset

Any valuable resource owned, controlled or managed by BHG.

Examples:

- intellectual property;
- software;
- brands;
- financial assets;
- knowledge;
- data.

---

# Naming Philosophy

Names inside the BHG ecosystem shall:

- communicate purpose;
- preserve meaning;
- support scalability;
- avoid temporary references;
- remain understandable over time.

---

# Prohibited Language Practices

The following practices are discouraged:

## Informal Institutional References

Avoid unofficial names for companies, systems or governance components.

---

## Ambiguous Ownership Terms

Avoid expressions that do not identify responsible ownership.

Examples:

- "the system"
- "the platform"
- "the team"

without additional context.

---

## Temporary Naming

Avoid names based only on:

- current technology;
- temporary projects;
- individual contributors.

---

# Artificial Intelligence Language Compatibility

Corporate language shall enable AI systems to correctly interpret institutional concepts.

AI systems may use this framework to:

- classify documents;
- resolve terminology;
- analyze relationships;
- detect inconsistencies.

AI systems shall not redefine official terminology.

---

# Documentation Language Rules

Official governance documentation shall:

- use approved terminology;
- maintain consistent naming;
- preserve defined concepts;
- avoid contradictory definitions.

---

# Evolution of Corporate Language

Language may evolve through controlled governance processes.

Changes require:

- documented proposal;
- impact evaluation;
- authority approval;
- historical registration.

---

# Relationship With Brand Identity

Corporate language supports:

- brand consistency;
- organizational alignment;
- communication quality;
- institutional recognition.

Language and brand identity shall evolve together.

---

# Compliance

All official BHG communications and documentation shall follow this corporate language framework.

---

# Institutional Principle

> Language creates shared understanding.

> Shared understanding creates institutional continuity.
