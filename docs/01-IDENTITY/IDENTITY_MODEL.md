# Identity Model

> Defines the institutional identity framework of Breto's Holding Group.

---

# Purpose

The Identity Model establishes the official framework that defines how Breto's Holding Group identifies itself across its entire ecosystem.

Identity provides coherence between:

- organizations;
- brands;
- products;
- technologies;
- documents;
- intellectual assets.

A strong institutional identity enables recognition, trust and continuity.

---

# Identity Definition

The identity of BHG represents the combination of:

- purpose;
- principles;
- values;
- capabilities;
- culture;
- knowledge;
- reputation.

Identity is not limited to visual representation.

Identity represents the complete institutional character of the ecosystem.

---

# Identity Layers

The BHG identity consists of multiple interconnected layers.

---

# Layer 1 — Institutional Identity

Defines what BHG represents as an institution.

Includes:

- mission;
- vision;
- principles;
- philosophy;
- legacy objectives.

Institutional identity provides strategic direction.

---

# Layer 2 — Organizational Identity

Defines how companies and organizational units belong to the ecosystem.

Includes:

- holding structure;
- subsidiaries;
- business units;
- strategic domains.

Organizational identity creates structural coherence.

---

# Layer 3 — Brand Identity

Defines how BHG entities communicate externally.

Includes:

- names;
- symbols;
- visual systems;
- communication principles.

Brand identity creates recognition.

---

# Layer 4 — Product Identity

Defines how products and services are identified.

Includes:

- product naming;
- positioning;
- ownership;
- ecosystem relationship.

Products represent institutional capabilities.

---

# Layer 5 — Technology Identity

Defines how technological assets are identified.

Includes:

- platforms;
- systems;
- architectures;
- digital infrastructure.

Technology identity preserves technical coherence.

---

# Layer 6 — Documentary Identity

Defines how institutional knowledge artifacts are identified.

Includes:

- documents;
- repositories;
- versions;
- identifiers;
- historical records.

Documentation represents institutional memory.

---

# Identity Principles

## Principle 1 — Consistency

Every BHG element shall maintain alignment with institutional identity.

---

## Principle 2 — Traceability

Every identity element shall have a clear origin and ownership.

---

## Principle 3 — Distinction

Each entity shall maintain a unique identity while preserving ecosystem alignment.

---

## Principle 4 — Evolution

Identity may evolve without losing foundational continuity.

---

## Principle 5 — Authenticity

Identity shall represent real capabilities and institutional commitments.

---

# Identity Relationships

BHG identity follows this structure:
BHG Institutional Identity

    ↓

Holding Identity

    ↓

Company Identity

    ↓

Brand Identity

    ↓

Product Identity

    ↓

Technology Identity

    ↓

Documentary Identity


Each level inherits alignment from the previous level.

---

# Source of Identity Authority

The authoritative identity sources are:

1. Foundation documents
2. Governance documents
3. Official brand documentation
4. Approved organizational records

Unofficial representations shall not override official identity definitions.

---

# Artificial Intelligence Compatibility

Authorized AI systems may use the Identity Model to:

- identify institutional entities;
- classify assets;
- detect naming conflicts;
- verify ecosystem alignment.

AI systems shall not create official identities.

Identity authority remains human governed.

---

# Identity Evolution

Identity changes require:

- documented proposal;
- impact analysis;
- governance review;
- historical registration.

Changes shall preserve institutional continuity.

---

# Compliance

All BHG entities, products, technologies and documents shall maintain alignment with this Identity Model.

Identity conflicts shall be resolved through governance processes.

---

# Institutional Principle

> Identity creates coherence.

> Institutions become recognizable when every element reflects the same underlying purpose.
