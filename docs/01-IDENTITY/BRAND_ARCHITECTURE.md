# Brand Architecture

> Defines the institutional brand architecture model of Breto's Holding Group.

---

# Purpose

Brand Architecture establishes how brands, subsidiaries, products and institutional identities are organized within the BHG ecosystem.

Its purpose is to preserve strategic coherence while allowing independent growth of business units and products.

A scalable ecosystem requires clear identity relationships.

---

# Brand Architecture Principles

The BHG brand system follows these principles:

## Institutional Coherence

Every brand belonging to BHG shall maintain alignment with the institutional identity and principles of the ecosystem.

---

## Strategic Independence

Business units may develop independent market identities while preserving ecosystem alignment.

---

## Clear Ownership

Every brand asset shall have:

- defined ownership;
- responsible authority;
- documented purpose;
- lifecycle management.

---

## Long-Term Preservation

Brand decisions shall consider:

- institutional continuity;
- future expansion;
- acquisitions;
- international growth.

---

# Corporate Brand

## Breto's Holding Group

BHG represents the institutional identity of the ecosystem.

The corporate brand establishes:

- trust;
- governance;
- ownership;
- institutional continuity.

The corporate brand shall not compete directly with operational products unless explicitly authorized.

---

# Business Unit Brands

Business units may maintain independent identities when:

- market positioning requires specialization;
- customer segments differ;
- operational independence provides strategic value.

Business unit brands remain part of the BHG ecosystem.

---

# Product Brands

Products may have their own identity when necessary.

Product branding shall:

- maintain ownership traceability;
- preserve institutional connection;
- avoid creating ambiguous ownership.

---

# Brand Relationship Models

The following models are authorized:

## Endorsed Brand Model

A separate brand supported by BHG endorsement.

Example:

Brand Name

"A BHG Company"

---

## Sub-Brand Model

A product or service operating under a primary brand.

Example:

BHG Brand

↓

Product Name

---

## Independent Brand Model

A fully independent market identity owned by BHG.

Used when strategic separation is required.

---

# Brand Creation Requirements

Every new brand proposal shall define:

- brand name;
- purpose;
- ownership;
- relationship with BHG;
- target market;
- strategic justification;
- lifecycle expectations.

---

# Brand Naming Governance

Brand names shall comply with:

- Corporate Naming Standards;
- Intellectual Property requirements;
- Legal ownership requirements;
- Ecosystem identity principles.

---

# Brand Ownership

All brands created inside the BHG ecosystem shall have:

- documented ownership;
- registered identity;
- assigned responsible authority;
- preservation strategy.

Ownership shall remain independent from temporary operators.

---

# Brand Evolution

Brands may evolve through:

- expansion;
- restructuring;
- acquisition;
- consolidation;
- retirement.

Every change shall preserve historical traceability.

---

# Artificial Intelligence Compatibility

AI systems may assist with:

- brand consistency analysis;
- naming evaluation;
- identity relationship mapping;
- documentation generation.

AI systems shall not:

- create official brands;
- assign ownership;
- approve brand changes.

Human governance remains the final authority.

---

# Governance Integration

Brand architecture integrates with:

- ecosystem governance;
- legal ownership;
- intellectual property management;
- repository governance.

Identity decisions shall remain traceable.

---

# Compliance

Any brand created within the BHG ecosystem shall comply with this architecture model.

Unauthorized brands or identities shall not represent official BHG entities.

---

# Institutional Principle

> Strong institutions require coherent identities.

> Brand architecture preserves recognition, ownership and strategic continuity across generations.
