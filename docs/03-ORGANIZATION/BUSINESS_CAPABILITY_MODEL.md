# Business Capability Model

> Defines the permanent institutional capabilities required by Breto's Holding Group to operate, scale and preserve value across generations.

---

# Purpose

The Business Capability Model establishes the strategic capabilities that enable BHG to fulfill its mission.

Capabilities represent what the organization must be able to do regardless of:

- specific products;
- technologies;
- companies;
- markets;
- temporary projects.

---

# Capability Philosophy

A business capability represents a permanent organizational ability.

Products may change.

Companies may evolve.

Markets may disappear.

Capabilities preserve institutional competence.

---

# Capability Principles

## Principle 1 — Capability Before Execution

BHG shall identify required capabilities before creating operational structures.

---

## Principle 2 — Long-Term Perspective

Capabilities shall be designed considering institutional continuity.

---

## Principle 3 — Separation Between Capability And Implementation

A capability defines:

- required ability;
- strategic purpose;
- ownership responsibility.

It does not define:

- specific software;
- specific company;
- specific product.

---

## Principle 4 — Capability Reusability

Shared capabilities may support multiple companies and domains.

---

The BHG capability model consists of strategic capability groups.

    |

    |
    +-- Knowledge Capabilities
    |

---

# 1. Governance Capabilities

Governance capabilities enable institutional coordination.

Examples:

- governance management;
- decision systems;
- compliance;
- audit;
- risk management;
- institutional documentation.

Purpose:

Preserve organizational integrity.

---

# 2. Capital Capabilities

Capital capabilities enable financial sustainability.

Examples:

- investment management;
- financial planning;
- treasury management;
- asset allocation;
- portfolio analysis.

Purpose:

Protect and increase institutional capital.

---

# 3. Technology Capabilities

Technology capabilities enable digital infrastructure.

Examples:

- software engineering;
- artificial intelligence;
- cybersecurity;
- cloud infrastructure;
- data systems;
- automation platforms.

Purpose:

Create scalable technological foundations.

---

# 4. Market Capabilities

Market capabilities enable value creation.

Examples:

- customer understanding;
- market research;
- branding;
- sales;
- partnerships;
- distribution.

Purpose:

Connect institutional capabilities with external ecosystems.

---

# 5. Operational Capabilities

Operational capabilities enable execution.

Examples:

- project management;
- process management;
- service delivery;
- quality management;
- resource coordination.

Purpose:

Transform strategy into execution.

---

# 6. Knowledge Capabilities

Knowledge capabilities preserve institutional intelligence.

Examples:

- documentation systems;
- research;
- education;
- knowledge management;
- organizational learning.

Purpose:

Prevent loss of institutional knowledge.

---

# 7. Innovation Capabilities

Innovation capabilities enable future growth.

Examples:

- experimentation;
- research and development;
- emerging technology analysis;
- venture creation.

Purpose:

Create future strategic opportunities.

---

# Capability Ownership

Every strategic capability shall have:

- responsible authority;
- governance owner;
- maturity level;
- evolution roadmap.

No capability shall exist without accountability.

---

# Capability Maturity

Capabilities may evolve through maturity levels:

## Level 1 — Defined

Capability identified and documented.

## Level 2 — Established

Capability has responsible ownership.

## Level 3 — Operational

Capability produces measurable outcomes.

## Level 4 — Scalable

Capability supports multiple entities.

## Level 5 — Institutionalized

Capability becomes a permanent organizational asset.

---

# Relationship With Companies

Companies within BHG consume and develop capabilities.

A company does not own the complete institutional capability unless explicitly assigned.

Capabilities belong to the ecosystem.

---

# Artificial Intelligence Support

AI systems may assist with:

- capability analysis;
- maturity evaluation;
- dependency mapping;
- strategic recommendations.

AI systems shall not define institutional ownership.

---

# Capability Evolution

Capabilities shall evolve through:

- strategic review;
- market analysis;
- technological evolution;
- governance decisions.

Changes shall preserve historical traceability.

---

# Compliance

Every major BHG initiative shall identify:

- required capabilities;
- responsible owners;
- capability dependencies.

Projects without capability alignment require governance review.

---

# Institutional Principle

> Companies execute.

> Capabilities endure.

> Institutional strength comes from the abilities preserved across generations.
