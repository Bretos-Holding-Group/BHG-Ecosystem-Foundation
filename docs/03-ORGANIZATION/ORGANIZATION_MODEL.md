# Organization Model

> Defines the official organizational architecture of Breto's Holding Group.

---

# Purpose

The Organization Model establishes how BHG structures itself to transform strategic vision into coordinated execution.

It defines the relationship between:

- holding structure;
- companies;
- business units;
- capabilities;
- operational teams;
- strategic functions.

---

# Organizational Philosophy

BHG is designed as an ecosystem organization.

The organization shall prioritize:

- strategic alignment;
- operational autonomy;
- scalable structures;
- clear accountability;
- long-term continuity.

---

# Organizational Principles

## Principle 1 — Purpose Driven Organization

Every organizational component shall exist because it contributes to BHG's mission and strategic objectives.

No organizational structure shall exist without defined purpose.

---

## Principle 2 — Clear Responsibility

Every organizational entity shall have:

- defined purpose;
- responsible authority;
- operational scope;
- measurable objectives.

Ambiguous ownership is prohibited.

---

## Principle 3 — Strategic Unity, Operational Autonomy

BHG entities shall maintain strategic alignment while preserving operational independence.

The holding provides:

- direction;
- governance;
- shared capabilities.

Operating entities provide:

- execution;
- specialization;
- market adaptation.

---

## Principle 4 — Capability Based Structure

Organizations shall be designed around capabilities rather than only departments.

Capabilities represent what BHG can consistently achieve.

---

## Principle 5 — Separation Between Ownership and Operation

Ownership structures and operational structures shall remain conceptually separated.

Ownership determines control rights.

Operations determine execution responsibilities.

---

# Organizational Levels

The BHG organizational model consists of multiple levels.

---

# Level 1 — Holding Level

The holding represents the highest organizational coordination layer.

Responsibilities:

- strategic direction;
- capital allocation;
- governance;
- portfolio management;
- institutional preservation.

---

# Level 2 — Subholding Level

Subholdings organize related business ecosystems.

Responsibilities:

- coordinate business domains;
- manage strategic portfolios;
- enable specialization.

---

# Level 3 — Company Level

Companies execute specific commercial or operational missions.

Responsibilities:

- products;
- services;
- customers;
- markets;
- operations.

---

# Level 4 — Business Unit Level

Business units represent functional or market-focused divisions.

Responsibilities:

- execution;
- innovation;
- operational performance.

---

# Level 5 — Team Level

Teams execute specialized activities.

Responsibilities:

- implementation;
- delivery;
- continuous improvement.

---

# Organizational Components

Every organizational component shall define:

## Identity

Including:

- name;
- purpose;
- classification;
- ownership relationship.

---

## Authority

Including:

- responsible governance;
- decision rights;
- accountability.

---

## Capabilities

Including:

- activities performed;
- resources required;
- outputs generated.

---

## Relationships

Including:

- dependencies;
- integrations;
- reporting structure.

---

# Organizational Independence

Entities within BHG may operate independently when they maintain:

- governance compliance;
- defined boundaries;
- documented ownership;
- strategic alignment.

---

# Organizational Integration

New organizations incorporated into BHG shall undergo evaluation of:

- strategic alignment;
- governance compatibility;
- capability contribution;
- operational integration.

---

# Organizational Evolution

The organization shall evolve according to:

- strategic needs;
- market conditions;
- technological opportunities;
- institutional objectives.

Changes shall preserve:

- traceability;
- historical records;
- governance alignment.

---

# Artificial Intelligence Support

Authorized AI systems may support organizational analysis through:

- capability mapping;
- organizational modeling;
- dependency analysis;
- maturity evaluation.

AI systems shall not define ownership or authority independently.

---

# Compliance

Every organizational component shall comply with this model.

Structures without defined purpose, ownership or responsibility shall be considered organizational inconsistencies.

---

# Institutional Principle

> Organizations provide structure.

> Structure enables coordination.

> Coordination transforms vision into institutional capability.
