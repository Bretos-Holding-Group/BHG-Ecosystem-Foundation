# Business Domain Model

> Defines the strategic business territories where Breto's Holding Group creates, manages and develops value.

---

# Purpose

The Business Domain Model establishes the official classification framework for the industries, markets and strategic areas where BHG may operate.

It provides a stable organizational map independent from specific companies or products.

---

# Domain Philosophy

A business domain represents a strategic territory.

Domains define:

- where BHG creates value;
- where capabilities are applied;
- where companies may be created;
- where investments may occur.

Domains do not define individual products.

---

# Domain Principles

## Principle 1 — Domain Before Company

A company shall exist within a defined strategic domain.

---

## Principle 2 — Long-Term Classification

Domains shall represent enduring strategic areas rather than temporary market opportunities.

---

## Principle 3 — Capability Alignment

Every domain shall require and consume specific organizational capabilities.

---

## Principle 4 — Controlled Expansion

New domains require strategic evaluation before ecosystem integration.

---

# 1. Financial Technology Domain

Purpose:

Develop financial infrastructure, digital payments, financial services and economic empowerment platforms.

Potential capabilities:

- fintech engineering;
- payment infrastructure;
- financial compliance;
- digital identity;
- transaction systems;
- financial analytics.

Potential entities:

- fintech companies;
- payment platforms;
- financial infrastructure services.

---

# 2. Artificial Intelligence Domain

Purpose:

Develop intelligent systems, automation platforms and AI-based organizational capabilities.

Potential capabilities:

- machine learning;
- AI engineering;
- automation;
- knowledge systems;
- intelligent agents.

Potential entities:

- AI platforms;
- AI services;
- automation companies.

---

# 3. Digital Infrastructure Domain

Purpose:

Create technological foundations supporting the ecosystem.

Potential capabilities:

- cloud systems;
- cybersecurity;
- software platforms;
- data infrastructure;
- developer platforms.

Potential entities:

- infrastructure companies;
- technology services.

---

# 4. Media & Creative Domain

Purpose:

Develop cultural, creative and communication ecosystems.

Potential capabilities:

- content creation;
- entertainment production;
- digital media;
- creative technology.

Potential entities:

- media companies;
- creative studios;
- digital platforms.

---

# 5. Consumer Services Domain

Purpose:

Develop products and services directly serving individuals and communities.

Potential capabilities:

- customer experience;
- distribution;
- commerce;
- service operations.

Potential entities:

- consumer brands;
- marketplaces;
- service companies.

---

# Future Strategic Domains

BHG may create additional domains when:

- strategic opportunity exists;
- required capabilities are available;
- governance approval is obtained;
- ecosystem alignment is demonstrated.

---

# Domain Ownership

Every business domain shall define:

- responsible governance authority;
- strategic owner;
- related capabilities;
- maturity level;
- development roadmap.

---

# Domain And Subholding Relationship

A subholding may organize one or multiple related domains.

Example:


BHG

|
+-- Technology Subholding

   |
   +-- AI Domain
   +-- Digital Infrastructure Domain

+-- Financial Subholding

   |
   +-- Financial Technology Domain

---

# Domain Maturity

Business domains evolve through maturity levels.

## Level 1 — Exploration

Domain identified as strategic opportunity.

## Level 2 — Development

Capabilities and opportunities evaluated.

## Level 3 — Operational

Companies operate inside the domain.

## Level 4 — Expansion

Multiple entities participate.

## Level 5 — Institutional Domain

Domain becomes a permanent ecosystem pillar.

---

# Artificial Intelligence Support

AI systems may assist with:

- domain analysis;
- market intelligence;
- opportunity evaluation;
- portfolio analysis;
- strategic simulations.

AI systems shall not:

- create official domains;
- authorize investments;
- modify ecosystem structure.

---

# Domain Evolution

Domain changes shall preserve:

- historical records;
- strategic rationale;
- governance approval;
- ecosystem consistency.

---

# Compliance

Every BHG company, initiative or investment shall identify its associated business domain.

Activities without domain alignment require governance review.

---

# Institutional Principle

> Companies operate inside domains.

> Domains organize capabilities.

> Capabilities preserve institutional strength.
